# Editorial Content

Establish audience, purpose, voice and supported claims. Use evidence-backed
structure, accessible headings and useful metadata. Separate content editing
from campaign launch, outreach, tracking changes or publishing. Validate
technical SEO assumptions against actual page evidence and current
authoritative sources; no invented traffic, conversion or ranking gains.
