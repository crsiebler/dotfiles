# Domain Research

Define the question, time period, jurisdiction/domain and decision. Search
primary sources first, track provenance and publication/event dates, test
contradictory evidence and distinguish observation from inference. Explain
sample and access limits. Legal or market conclusions require current, relevant
sources; a persona is not a credential. Research grants no outreach,
subscription, transaction or external publication authority.
