# Research Methods

Choose interviews, observation, usability tasks, surveys or existing analytics
from the question and population. Define recruitment, consent, privacy, bias
and accessibility constraints. Separate observed behavior, participant
statements and interpretation. Synthesize actionable evidence with confidence
and limitations. No implied contacting, recording, recruiting or incentive
spending.
