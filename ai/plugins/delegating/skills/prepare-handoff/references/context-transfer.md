# Context Transfer

Identify the consumer and decision. Prioritize objective, constraints, actual
state, decisions with reasons, evidence, blockers and next actions. Preserve
source provenance and uncertainty; minimize secrets and irrelevant personal
data. Do not invent storage or context APIs, persistent memory, delivery
receipts or freshness. The main session reconciles canonical state and
ownership.
