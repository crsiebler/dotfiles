# Architecture Assessment

Evaluate decisions against actual constraints, alternatives, coupling,
compatibility, lifecycle and recovery evidence. Identify concrete consequences
and tradeoffs; no mandatory pattern or architecture ceremony. Preserve
inspection-only scope and distinguish recommendations from proven defects.
