# Payments And Finance

Preserve money precision, ledger invariants and reconciliation. Model
transaction states, idempotency, retries, webhook verification, ordering and
partial failures. Keep secrets and payment data out of logs and fixtures. Use
approved test environments; no implicit charging, refunds, account access or
regulatory-certification claims. Confirm jurisdiction/provider/version details
from authoritative evidence.
