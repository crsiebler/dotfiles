# Model Lifecycle

Define task, dataset provenance, supported model/runtime and acceptance before
training or deployment. Separate training, validation and held-out data;
inspect leakage, bias and relevant failure modes. Preserve reproducible
model/data/prompt versions. Measure serving latency, cost, accuracy and
robustness on representative workloads, including retries/caches. Plan drift
detection, fallback and rollback. No implicit model downloads, training spend,
production rollout or invented benchmark gains.
