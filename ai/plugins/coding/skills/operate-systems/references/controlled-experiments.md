# Controlled Experiments

Require explicit targets, authorized techniques, window, blast radius, stop
conditions, recovery owner and evidence handling before active experiments.
Planning or assessment alone authorizes no scanning, exploitation, fault
injection, privilege changes or live remediation. Preserve protections,
credentials and unrelated resources; stop on unexpected effects and report
evidence rather than escalating activity.
