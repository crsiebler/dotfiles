# Incidents

Triage impact and preserve timeline/evidence before changes. Separate observed
symptoms, hypotheses and confirmed causes. Choose authorized containment or
recovery steps, define blast radius and stop conditions, verify service and
user-path recovery, and record unresolved risks. Keep communications scoped and
authorized; observation does not grant standing remediation authority.
